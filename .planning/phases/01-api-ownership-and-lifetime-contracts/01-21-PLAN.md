<objective>
Close the two Phase 1 verification gaps on the lifecycle and thread-affinity side, plus review finding WR-06, as the first part of the gap-closure release voyahslam 0.9.2 (user decision 2026-10-21):

1. Gap 1 (major, OWN-04, review WR-01): `EventNode::StopWith` reaches the own-thread refusal only from the Running case, so a worker that calls its component's `Stop()` while another thread is already stopping it deadlocks. Move the check in front of the state switch, for every state, and make every component pass its worker's `OnWorkerThread()`.

Purpose: the phase goal says wrong-thread calls must fail loudly, not hang, and that every thread-affine entry point is enforced; the examples are what users copy.
